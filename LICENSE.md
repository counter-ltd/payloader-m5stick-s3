Preamble

Payloader exists to expand human autonomy, privacy, and security literacy
through open, inspectable, user-owned hardware firmware.

This project was created with the belief that security tooling should be
understandable — not a black box. People deserve to know how their
authentication devices work, how IR signals are structured, how Bluetooth
pairing happens under the hood. Payloader is intended to remain hackable
for beginners, extensible for experts, and transparent for everyone.

The firmware is provided freely in the hope that it may help others learn,
build, and take ownership of the hardware in their hands.
